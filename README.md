## What is the Data to Decision Series?
**Data to Decision** is an ongoing collection of focused analytics case studies exploring how data answers everyday business and behavioral questions — from pricing and demand signals to KPIs, forecasting, and optimization.

Instead of massive dashboards, this series emphasizes **small, well-executed analyses** that demonstrate analytical clarity, reproducibility, and communication skill.

---

## Purpose
- Translate raw datasets into **clear, decision-oriented insights**
- Practice end-to-end analytics workflows (clean → analyze → interpret)
- Build a **public, reproducible portfolio** of analytical thinking
- Demonstrate both **technical depth and communication ability**
- Develop consistency through weekly or bi-weekly case studies

---

## Philosophy
Not every project needs to be huge.  
Well-structured micro-analyses often reveal sharper insights than oversized dashboards.

**Clarity > Complexity**  
**Consistency > Perfection**  
**Insight > Volume**

---

## Case Study Framework
Each case study generally follows a consistent structure:

1. **Question Framing** – What decision are we informing?
2. **Data Preparation** – Cleaning, merging, feature engineering
3. **Exploratory Analysis** – Patterns, distributions, anomalies
4. **Multivariate Checks** – Interactions between variables
5. **Optional Modeling** – Feature importance, forecasting, or classification
6. **Findings & Limitations** – Honest, non-overclaimed conclusions
7. **Visual Storytelling** – Clean, decision-focused charts
